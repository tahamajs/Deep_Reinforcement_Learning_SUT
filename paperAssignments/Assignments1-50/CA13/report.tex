\begin{abstract}
We introduce SimGolf-Latent, a method that integrates simulation-based global
search (SimGolf) with learned latent world models (DreamerV3). By maintaining a
buffer of latent checkpoints and performing imagined branching search from
critical decision latents, our approach reduces planning error in long-horizon,
sparse-reward navigation tasks. We provide theoretical justification, algorithmic
details, and empirical evaluation protocols.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Model-based RL with latent world models enables sample-efficient planning.
However, long-horizon planning under partial observability and sparse rewards
remains challenging. SimGolf introduces episodic simulator resets and branching
to escape local optima. We adapt SimGolf to operate purely in latent space,
leveraging DreamerV3's RSSM to imagine resets and branches without environment
resets.

\section{Method}
\subsection{Latent Dynamics}
Let the RSSM define a stochastic latent \(z_t\), with learned transition
\(p_\theta(z_{t+1}\!\mid\!z_t,a_t)\). A decoder predicts observations and
rewards. We use the actor \(\pi_\phi(a\mid z)\) and value \(v_\psi(z)\) in
latent space.

\subsection{SimGolf Branching}
Given a checkpoint latent \(z_s\), generate \(B\) branches of horizon \(H\).
Each branch \(b\) yields rewards \(r_{s,b,0:H-1}\) and discounts \(\gamma_{s,b,0:H-1}\).
We compute bootstrapped returns
\[
\hat R_b \;=\; \sum_{k=0}^{H-1}\Big(\prod_{j=0}^{k-1}\gamma_{s,b,j}\Big) r_{s,b,k}
 \Big(\prod_{j=0}^{H-1}\gamma_{s,b,j}\Big) v_\psi(z_{s,b,H}).
\]
Top-k aggregation or soft weighting produces action targets for policy update.

\subsection{TD(\(\lambda\)) Targets from Imagined Branches}
For imagined sequence \(\{(z_t,r_t,\gamma_t)\}\) we compute TD(\(\lambda\)) targets:
\[
G_t = r_t + \gamma_t\big((1-\lambda)v_\psi(z_{t+1}) + \lambda G_{t+1}\big),
\]
and minimize \(\|v_\psi(z_t)-\mathrm{stopgrad}(G_t)\|^2\).

\section{Planner Integration}
We describe software hooks: checkpoint buffer, trigger module (TD-error, uncertainty,
entropy), and the branching simulator that uses either policy sampling or CEM
to propose action sequences. We include an uncertainty penalty \(r' = r - \kappa\sigma_r\)
when reward variance \(\sigma_r\) is available.

\section{Experiments}
We recommend evaluating on D4RL AntMaze and custom maze tasks with metrics:
success rate, steps-to-goal, planner overhead, and model bias (predicted vs real reward).
Run 5 seeds, report mean±std and IQM.

\section{Related Work}
DreamerV3, SimGolf, CEM-MPC, and prioritized replay literature are discussed.

\section{Conclusion}
Latent SimGolf combines global search with Dreamer-style imagination to refine
policies at critical decision points. We provide an implementation scaffold and
experiments protocol for reproducible research.
